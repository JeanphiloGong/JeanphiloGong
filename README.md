## Selected Work

### [Lens](https://github.com/JeanphiloGong/tsingai-lens)

**Research software / In development**

A research workspace for investigating questions across collections of papers:
inspect original sources, compare experimental results with their conditions,
and trace findings back to the evidence. Materials science is the first focus.
